## FINAL VERDICT

### ✅ PhoneArena WINS: 10/12 categories

**Why PhoneArena is better:**
1. ✅ **Better organization** - Design (vs Body), Hardware (merge Platform+Memory), Multimedia (vs Sound)
2. ✅ **Better structure** - Camera split clearly, Connectivity+Features merge well
3. ✅ **More complete** - Regulatory separate, Benchmarks detailed, Charge speed separate
4. ✅ **Better naming** - Cellular (vs Network), Design (vs Body), Multimedia (vs Sound)
5. ✅ **Better UX** - Prices in Availability, Colors in Design, SIM in Cellular

### 🟡 GSMArena advantages:
1. ✅ 2G/3G bands (nhưng ít cần thiết)
2. ✅ EU Label category (mới, chưa phổ biến)
3. ✅ Technology field trong Network
